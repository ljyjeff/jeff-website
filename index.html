What it does

  • Nag alarms talk.A character says something new every time the alarm rings. An AI on your iPhone writes her lines and speaks them.
  • It can lie about the time.On a Fun alarm, the voice may say it's later than it is, to get you moving. You can switch that off on any alarm.
  • Fun alarms learn.Set the time you need to be ready by. After three alarms, a Fun alarm can ring early so you're ready on time.
  • Stop doesn't end it.Stop it on the lock screen and it rings again after its snooze time. Only DISMISS in the app ends it.
