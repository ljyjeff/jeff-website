Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to mistimed@ljyjeff.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what the alarm did.

Why does my alarm keep coming back?

That's how Mistimed works. Stop it on the lock screen, with any button, and it rings again after its snooze time, every time. To end it, open Mistimed and tap DISMISS. Switching the alarm off, or deleting it, ends it too. A repeating alarm stays on for its next day.

How does snooze work?

In Mistimed, every stop is a snooze: press Stop or any button and the alarm rings again after its snooze time. Each alarm's snooze time is in its editor, from 1 to 15 minutes, 10 to start.

If your iPhone is unlocked when the alarm rings, it shows as a banner: tap Stop there, or open Mistimed, so it comes back on time.

Why did my alarm ring early?

It's a Fun alarm. After three alarms, a Fun alarm learns how long you take to get going and can ring a little early, so you're ready by the time you set. When one will ring early, its row shows a Mistimed tag: tap it to see when, or turn on Always show early ring details in Settings. To make an alarm ring exactly at its time, open it and pick Boring.

Why did she say it's later than it is?

That's Lie about time. On a Fun alarm with a Nag voice, the character may say a later time than it is, to get you moving. How much comes from how late you really run. The screen always shows the real time. To turn it off, open the alarm and switch off Lie about time.

How do I get the Nag voices?

Tap the gear icon for Settings, then Download under Nag voices. They take about 1.1 GB and download in the background, and Settings shows how far along they are. Once they're here, pick Nag as an alarm's sound. To free the space, tap Remove in the same place; Nag alarms then ring the default tone.

Why did my alarm ring a tone instead of a voice?

Nag alarms use the default tone until the Nag voices are downloaded. The tone is also there as a backup, so the alarm always rings on time.

Want to tell us about a line?

Every line is made up on the spot by an AI on your phone, so no two rings are the same. If one misses the mark, tell us what it said and when, and we'll use it to make her better.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 27 or later. The Nag voices need about 1.1 GB of free space.
